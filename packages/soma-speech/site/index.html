SOMA Speech

A microphone button for any text box. Click it, talk, and your words go in where the cursor is. Click again, or press Esc, to stop.

Try it

The mic sits next to the box. Type something, put the cursor in the middle of it, then dictate. Your typing stays, and the new words go in at the cursor.

    Words you are still saying show above the mic, not in the box, so pressing Enter can never send half a sentence.

    If you are building the app: choose the voice

    The people who use your app see only a mic. You choose which voice sits behind it, with one setting. Try both here. The two boxes above switch with your choice.

    Add it to your own app

    One line, anywhere on the page. The mic appears next to the box that matches data-target.

    <script src="https://soma-speech.netlify.app/soma-speech.js"
        data-target="#chat-input" defer></script>

    For the punctuated voice add data-voice="punctuated" and data-scribe-token-url, as shown above. For a box your app draws later, call SomaSpeech.attach('#chat-input') once it exists. The package, its tests and the design notes are in soma-platform/packages/soma-speech.
